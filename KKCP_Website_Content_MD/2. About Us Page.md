**ABOUT US Page**

Established in 1992, K.K. College of Pharmacy experienced significant development under the leadership of Prof. K.R. Arumugam, a distinguished educator and academic who served in prestigious roles as a member of the Senate and Governing Council for the Tamilnadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University. Initially providing Diploma and Degree courses in Pharmacy, the institution quickly attained full-fledged recognition as a Pharmacy College celebrated for its Masters and Ph.D. programs, all located in the charming city of Chennai. The campus features a tranquil setting positioned at Gerugambakkam on Porur-Kundrathur Road.

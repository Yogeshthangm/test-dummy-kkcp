**Scholarship Page**

**Pudhumai Penn Scheme**

The Pudhumai Penn Scheme provides monetary assistance to girls who have passed out from Government and Government-aided educational institutions to pursue higher studies. It aims to improve the general well-being of female students and encourages them to continue their higher education with financial assistance of ₹1,000 per month.

**First Graduate Scholarship**

The First Graduate (FG) Scholarship is a flagship program implemented by the Government of Tamil Nadu for students who are the first in their immediate family to pursue higher education. This concession applies to B.Pharm students admitted under the Government Quota, with financial assistance of ₹18,000 per year for the entire course duration.

**SC/ST Scholarship for B.Pharm Students**

SC/ST students pursuing a B.Pharm degree are primarily supported through the Centrally Sponsored Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme. To avail this scholarship, the annual family income from all sources should not exceed ₹2.50 lakh.

The applicant must belong to a recognized Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Caste Converted to Christianity (SCC), or Scheduled Tribe (ST) category. The scholarship covers a tuition fee of ₹43,000 per year, along with maintenance fees as applicable.

**SC/ST Scholarship for Pharm.D Students**

Eligible SC/ST students pursuing a Pharm.D degree can apply for the Central Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST Students. The scholarship is available to Indian national students belonging to SC/ST categories studying at recognized institutions. Parents\' annual income must generally not exceed ₹2,50,000.

The scholarship covers ₹2 lakh per year and maintenance fees as applicable for Government Quota students. For Management Quota students, it covers ₹5,25,000 per year and maintenance fees as applicable.

**Tamil Puthalvan Scheme**

The Tamil Puthalvan Scheme is a flagship welfare initiative of the Government of Tamil Nadu that provides eligible male students with monthly financial assistance of ₹1,000 to support their higher education. The scheme benefits students who have completed Classes 6 to 12 in Government or Government-aided schools, helping them pursue their academic goals and build a brighter future.

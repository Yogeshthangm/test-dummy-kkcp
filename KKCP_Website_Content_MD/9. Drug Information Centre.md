**Drug Information Centre**

The institution has a well-established Drug Information Centre (DIC) located within Dr. Kamakshi Memorial Hospital, Pallikaranai, a tertiary care facility. The centre is equipped with a comprehensive library, high-performance computer systems, and advanced clinical decision-support resources, including Lexicomp software.

The centre serves as a valuable resource for providing evidence-based drug information, assisting in clinical decision-making, addressing drug-related queries from both healthcare professionals and patients, and supporting individualized dose and dosage adjustment recommendations. It plays a vital role in promoting rational drug use and enhancing patient care through access to reliable and up-to-date pharmaceutical information.

The Drug Information Centre also supports academic and professional development by facilitating journal clubs, case presentations, and continuous learning activities. Under the guidance of experienced Clinical Pharmacists and specialized Medical Professionals, students gain practical exposure to drug information services and evidence-based pharmacy practice.

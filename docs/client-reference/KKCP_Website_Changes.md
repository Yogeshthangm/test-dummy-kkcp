  ```
  *Scholarship Page*

  Pudhumai Penn scheme
  The Pudhumai Penn scheme provides monetary assistance to girls who have passed
  out from government and government-aided educational institutions to pursue
  higher studies. It aims to improve the general well-being of female students and
  encourages them to continue their higher education with a financial assistance of
  Rs. 1000 every month.

  The First Graduate scholarship
  The First Graduate (FG) Scholarship is a flagship program implemented by the
  Tamil Nadu government for students who are the first in their immediate family to
  pursue higher education. This concession applies to B.Pharm students admitted
  under the Government Quota with a financial assistance of Rs.18000 per year for
  the entire course duration.

  SC/ST Scholarship for B.Pharm Students
  SC/ST students pursuing a B.Pharm degree are primarily supported through the
  Centrally Sponsored Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme. To avail this scholarship, the
  annual family income from all sources should not exceed ₹2.50 lakh.
  The applicant must belong to a recognized Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Caste
  Converted to Christianity (SCC), or Scheduled Tribe (ST) category. The scholarship
  covers a tuition fee of ₹43,000/year, along with maintenance fees as applicable.

  SC/ST SCHOLARSHIP FOR PHARM.D students
  Eligible SC/ST students pursuing a Pharm.D degree can apply for the Central
  Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST students. The scholarship is available to Indian
  national students belonging to SC/ST categories studying at recognized institutions.
  Parents' annual income must generally not exceed Rs. 2,50,000. The scholarship
  covers Rs. 2 lakh/year and maintenance fees as applicable for government quota
  students. For management quota students, it covers Rs. 5,25,000/year and
  maintenance fees as applicable.

  Tamil Puthalvan Scheme
  The Tamil Puthalvan Scheme is a flagship welfare initiative of the Government of
  Tamil Nadu that provides eligible male students with a monthly financial assistance
  of ₹1,000 to support their higher education. The scheme benefits students who have
  completed Classes 6 to 12 in Government or Government-aided schools, helping them
  pursue their academic goals and build a brighter future.
  ```
